## Title

Learn Japanese from Any Story — Greenlight Local, an AI Anime Director with NVIDIA Nemotron

## Description

Drop in a story. Get an anime you can learn a language from: directed by NVIDIA Nemotron, rendered on your own computer, and checked shot by shot before anyone watches it.

I grew up watching anime, and learning Japanese I got stuck in the middle: textbooks were too easy, real shows too fast, and almost nothing had readings over the kanji. So I started making my own study anime. Greenlight Local packages everything I learned into one app.

What you'll see:
• Nemotron directs every scene against a rulebook; code gates send any draft that breaks a rule back with the exact reason, and a second Nemotron reviews the cut as a producer
• Every rendered shot is measured: is the line spoken correctly, is there extra speech, is there room at the end
• When a shot fails, Nemotron diagnoses it from a library of proven causes, cites the evidence, and lays out every fix with its real cost
• Study mode: every line rewritten the way the character would really say it, with readings over the kanji, two subtitles, and a note on why it is said that way
• The whole app works in English, Chinese and Japanese
